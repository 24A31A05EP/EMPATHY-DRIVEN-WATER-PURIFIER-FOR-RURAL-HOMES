Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services — answered simply.

How quickly can a technician arrive at my village? +

We guarantee a technician within 24 hours of your service request, even in remote villages across all districts we serve. For emergency repairs, we prioritise your booking at the top of the queue.

Do you use genuine replacement filters? +

Yes, absolutely. We use only BIS-certified, OEM-grade replacement filters and parts. We never use third-party or duplicate components that compromise water quality.

Can I pay in instalments for installations? +

Yes! We offer EMI plans starting at just ₹199/month for installations. There are no hidden interest charges and no documentation hassle for basic plans.

What areas do you cover? +

We currently serve 500+ villages across Karnataka, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana. We're expanding every month — call us to check if your area is covered.

Is there a warranty on services performed? +

All repair and maintenance work comes with a 90-day service warranty. If the same issue recurs within that period, we'll re-attend at no extra charge.

How do I cancel or reschedule a booking? +

You can cancel or reschedule up to 12 hours before your appointment via your dashboard or by calling our toll-free number 1800-123-4567. No cancellation fees apply.
